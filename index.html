Emergency Crisis Protocols
Adrenaline (Epinephrine 1:10,000)--
Cardiac Arrest: 10 mcg/kg IV (0.1 mL/kg of 1:10,000). Dilution: 1 mL of 1:1,000 in 9 mL NS = 100 mcg/mL.
Atropine (Bradycardia)--
Dose: 0.02 mg/kg IV (Minimum dose: 0.1 mg to prevent paradoxical bradycardia; Max single: 0.5 mg).
Ephedrine (Hypotension)--
Dose: 0.1 - 0.2 mg/kg IV. Dilution: 30 mg ampoule in 9 mL NS = 3 mg/mL.
Hydrocortisone--
Dose: 2 - 4 mg/kg IV for acute status asthmaticus, anaphylaxis, or perioperative stress cover.
Succinylcholine (Laryngospasm)--
IV Dose: 1 - 2 mg/kg IV. IM Dose (no IV access): 3 - 4 mg/kg IM (always administer with Atropine 0.02 mg/kg).
