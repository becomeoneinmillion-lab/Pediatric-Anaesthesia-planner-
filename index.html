Emergency Drug Guidelines & Crisis Protocols
1. ADRENALINE (EPINEPHRINE) CRISIS PROTOCOLS
Cardiac Arrest (IV/IO): --
Dose: 10 mcg/kg (0.1 mL/kg of 1:10,000 dilution) every 3–5 min.
Prep: 1 mL ampoule (1:1,000 = 1 mg/mL) + 9 mL NS = 100 mcg/mL.
Anaphylaxis:
• IM Anterolateral Thigh: -- (10 mcg/kg of 1:1,000, 0.01 mL/kg; Max 0.3 mg child, 0.5 mg adolescent).
• IV Refractory / Hypotension: -- (1 mcg/kg bolus = 0.1 mL/kg of 1:100,000 dilute solution).
• Endotracheal (if no IV/IO): -- (50–100 mcg/kg = 0.5–1.0 mL/kg of 1:10,000 followed by 5 mL flush).
2. ACUTE INTRAOPERATIVE COMPLICATIONS
Laryngospasm Protocol:
1. 100% FiO2, CPAP + jaw thrust (Larson's notch pressure).
2. Propofol 0.5–1 mg/kg IV.
3. If persistent: Succinylcholine: -- (IV 1–2 mg/kg | IM 3–4 mg/kg with Atropine 0.02 mg/kg).
Severe Bronchospasm Protocol:
1. Deepen anaesthesia (Sevoflurane / Propofol / Ketamine 1 mg/kg).
2. Salbutamol MDI via ETT: 4–8 puffs.
3. Hydrocortisone: -- (4 mg/kg IV).
4. Refractory: Salbutamol IV 5 mcg/kg bolus or Adrenaline IV 1 mcg/kg.
Local Anaesthetic Systemic Toxicity (LAST):
• Stop LA injection, secure airway with 100% O2, hyperventilate.
• 20% Intralipid: -- (1.5 mL/kg IV bolus over 1 min; then 0.25 mL/kg/min infusion). Repeat bolus twice if unstable.
