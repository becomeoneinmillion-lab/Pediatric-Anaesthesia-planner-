Emergency Protocols & Dilutions
Adrenaline (1:10,000)--
10 mcg/kg IV (0.1 mL/kg of 1:10,000). Dilute 1 mL (1:1000) in 9 mL NS.
Atropine--
0.02 mg/kg IV (Min: 0.1 mg, Max single: 0.5 mg).
Ephedrine--
0.1 - 0.2 mg/kg IV. Dilute 30 mg ampoule in 9 mL NS (3 mg/mL).
Hydrocortisone--
2 - 4 mg/kg IV (Anaphylaxis / Status Asthmaticus / Stress dose).
Succinylcholine (Laryngospasm)--
IV: 1-2 mg/kg | IM: 3-4 mg/kg (always combine with Atropine 0.02 mg/kg).
